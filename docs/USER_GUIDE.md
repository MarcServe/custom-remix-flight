## Introduction

### What is LeadBoosters CRM?

LeadBoosters CRM is an AI-powered customer relationship management platform designed to help sales teams discover, engage, and convert leads more effectively. It combines traditional CRM functionality with advanced AI capabilities for lead discovery, email automation, and intelligent response generation.

### Key Features

- **AI-Powered Lead Discovery**: Find qualified leads using advanced AI search across multiple data sources
- **Intelligent Email Sequences**: Automated, personalized email campaigns with AI-generated content
- **Auto-Response System**: AI-powered email responses with human review
- **Comprehensive CRM**: Manage companies, contacts, deals, and activities in one place
- **Multi-Channel Email**: Connect Gmail, Outlook, SMTP, or verified email providers
- **Team Collaboration**: Shared inbox, role-based permissions, and team assignments
- **Real-Time Insights**: Live engagement tracking and detailed analytics
- **Invoice Management**: Create and send professional invoices directly from the CRM

### Who Should Use LeadBoosters CRM?

- **Sales Teams**: Streamline prospecting and outreach
- **Business Development**: Discover and qualify new opportunities
- **Marketing Teams**: Run targeted email campaigns
- **Founders & Solopreneurs**: Manage customer relationships efficiently
- **Account Managers**: Track deals and maintain client relationships

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the LeadBoosters CRM login page
2. Click "Sign Up" to create a new account
3. Enter your email address and create a secure password
4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. Complete your profile setup

### First-Time Setup

After creating your account, complete these essential setup steps:

1. **Business Profile**
   - Navigate to Settings → Business Profile
   - Add your company name, website, and description
   - Upload your company logo
   - Configure your timezone and preferences

2. **Email Integration**
   - Go to Integrations → Email Providers
   - Connect your primary email account (Gmail, Outlook, or SMTP)
   - Verify your sending domain for better deliverability

3. **Team Setup** (if applicable)
   - Navigate to Teams
   - Invite team members with appropriate roles
   - Configure permissions and assignments

### Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard provides an at-a-glance view of your sales activities:

- **Key Metrics**: Total leads, active deals, deal value, win rate
- **Email Activity Widget**: Recent email sends and engagement
- **Live Engagement Tracker**: Real-time email opens and clicks
- **Quick Actions**: Fast access to common tasks
- **Upcoming Events**: Calendar view of scheduled activities

## Lead Finder

### Overview

Lead Finder is an AI-powered tool that discovers qualified business leads based on your search criteria. It searches across multiple data sources and enriches company information with contacts, executive details, and social profiles.

### Starting a Lead Search

1. Navigate to **Lead Finder** from the sidebar
2. Enter your search criteria:
   - **Search Query**: Describe your ideal customer (e.g., "SaaS companies in healthcare")
   - **Max Results**: Number of leads to find (10-50)
   - **Location** (optional): Geographic targeting
   - **Industry** (optional): Specific industry focus

3. Click **Find Leads** to start the AI search

### Search Process

The Lead Finder performs multiple steps:

1. **Company Discovery**: AI identifies relevant companies
2. **Data Enrichment**: Gathers company details, website, social profiles
3. **Contact Finding**: Discovers key decision-makers and their contact information
4. **Quality Scoring**: Rates lead quality based on data completeness and relevance

### Understanding Lead Cards

Each lead card displays:

- **Company Name & Logo**: Visual identification
- **Industry & Location**: Quick context
- **Quality Score**: Star rating (1-5 stars) based on data completeness
- **Data Completeness Bar**: Visual indicator of available information
- **Source Badges**: Which data sources provided information
- **Contact Count**: Number of contacts discovered
- **Key Executives**: Names and titles of decision-makers

### Quality Scoring System

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **5 Stars**: Complete data, verified contacts, LinkedIn profiles
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4 Stars**: Good data, most contacts, social profiles
- ⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars**: Basic data, some contacts
- ⭐⭐ **2 Stars**: Limited data, few contacts
- ⭐ **1 Star**: Minimal data, needs manual enrichment

### Saving Leads

1. Review lead cards in the results
2. Click **Save to CRM** on individual leads
3. Or use **Save All** to import all discovered leads
4. Leads are automatically added to the Companies section
5. Contacts are linked to their respective companies

### Managing Active Searches

- View ongoing searches in the **Active Searches** sidebar
- Cancel searches that are taking too long
- Re-run failed searches with adjusted parameters
- Each search is tracked with a unique session ID

### Tips for Better Results

- **Be Specific**: "B2B SaaS companies in New York with 10-50 employees" works better than "tech companies"
- **Use Industry Filters**: Narrow down to specific industries for better targeting
- **Limit Results**: Start with 10-20 leads to test quality before running larger searches
- **Review Quality**: Focus on 3+ star leads for the best contact information

## Deals Pipeline

### Overview

Track sales opportunities through your pipeline from first contact to closed-won.

### Pipeline Stages

Default pipeline stages:
1. **Lead**: Initial contact or prospect
2. **Qualified**: Lead meets criteria and shows interest
3. **Proposal**: Formal proposal or quote sent
4. **Negotiation**: Terms and pricing discussion
5. **Closed Won**: Deal successfully closed
6. **Closed Lost**: Opportunity lost

### Creating Deals

1. Navigate to **Deals** or **Pipeline**
2. Click **+ Create Deal**
3. Fill in deal details:
   - **Deal Name**: Descriptive title
   - **Company**: Associated account
   - **Contact**: Primary contact for the deal
   - **Value**: Expected revenue
   - **Expected Close Date**: Target close date
   - **Stage**: Current pipeline stage
   - **Probability**: Win probability (%)
   - **Description**: Deal notes
4. Click **Create Deal**

### Kanban Board View (Pipeline)

The Pipeline page shows a visual Kanban board:

- **Drag & Drop**: Move deals between stages
- **Stage Columns**: Each pipeline stage has a column
- **Deal Cards**: Show deal name, value, company, and close date
- **Activity Indicators**: Visual cues for recent activity
- **Quick Actions**: Edit or delete deals from cards

### List View (Deals)

The Deals page shows a table view:

- **Sortable Columns**: Click headers to sort
- **Filters**: Filter by stage, company, date range
- **Search**: Find deals by name or company
- **Export**: Download deal data as CSV

### Deal Details

Click on any deal to view:

**Overview:**
- Deal information (value, stage, probability)
- Associated company and contact
- Expected close date and actual close date
- Owner/assigned team member

**Activities:**
- Email history
- Events and tasks
- Notes and comments
- Stage change history

**Actions:**
- **Send Email**: Quick email to deal contact
- **Create Event**: Schedule follow-up
- **Add Note**: Internal team notes
- **Update Stage**: Move to next stage
- **Mark Won/Lost**: Close the deal

### Deal Metrics

View key metrics on the Dashboard:
- **Total Deal Value**: Sum of all active deals
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of closed-won deals
- **Average Deal Size**: Mean value of deals
- **Deals by Stage**: Distribution across pipeline

### Best Practices

- **Update Regularly**: Keep deal stages current
- **Set Realistic Close Dates**: Adjust as circumstances change
- **Add Notes**: Document conversations and decisions
- **Track Activities**: Log all interactions
- **Review Weekly**: Pipeline review meetings

## Email Integration

### Overview

LeadBoosters CRM supports multiple email providers for seamless email integration.

### Supported Email Providers

1. **Gmail Direct OAuth**: Direct integration with Gmail accounts
2. **Outlook OAuth**: Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com accounts
3. **SendGrid**: High-volume transactional email
4. **Resend**: Modern email API
5. **Custom SMTP**: Any SMTP server
6. **Verified Email**: Pre-verified sending addresses

### Connecting Gmail

1. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Email Providers**
2. Click **Connect** on the Gmail Direct card
3. Click **Sign in with Google**
4. Authorize LeadBoosters to access your Gmail account
5. Grant required permissions:
   - Read emails
   - Send emails
   - Modify labels
6. Connection confirmed - you can now send emails

### Connecting Outlook

1. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Email Providers**
2. Click **Connect** on the Outlook card
3. Click **Connect Outlook**
4. Sign in with your Microsoft account
5. Authorize the integration
6. Connection confirmed

### Connecting SMTP

1. Click **Connect** on the Custom SMTP card
2. Enter SMTP server details:
   - **Host**: SMTP server address (e.g., smtp.gmail.com)
   - **Port**: Usually 587 (TLS) or 465 (SSL)
   - **Username**: Your email address
   - **Password**: App password or account password
   - **Secure**: Enable TLS/SSL
3. Click **Test Connection** to verify settings
4. If successful, click **Save**

### Connecting SendGrid

1. Obtain SendGrid API key from SendGrid dashboard
2. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Email Providers**
3. Click **Connect** on SendGrid card
4. Enter your API key
5. Click **Verify** to test connection
6. Save configuration

### Connecting Resend

1. Get Resend API key from Resend dashboard
2. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Email Providers**
3. Click **Connect** on Resend card
4. Enter API key
5. Test and save

### Email Deliverability

**Check Deliverability:**
1. Navigate to **Email Deliverability**
2. Enter the email address you want to verify
3. Click **Check Deliverability**
4. Review results:
   - DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
   - MX records
   - Blacklist status
   - Spam score

**Verify Domain:**
1. Add DNS records to your domain:
   - SPF record
   - DKIM record
   - DMARC record
2. Wait for DNS propagation (up to 48 hours)
3. Run deliverability check to confirm

**Best Practices:**
- Always verify your sending domain
- Maintain good sender reputation
- Avoid spam trigger words
- Include unsubscribe links
- Monitor bounce rates

### Managing Connected Accounts

**View Connections:**
- All connected accounts shown in Email Providers
- Status indicator (Connected/Disconnected)
- Last sync timestamp

**Disconnect Account:**
1. Click disconnect button on provider card
2. Confirm disconnection
3. Account removed (can be reconnected later)

**Default Provider:**
- Select your preferred default provider
- Used for all outbound emails unless specified otherwise
- Can override per email or sequence

## Sequences

### Overview

Email sequences are automated, personalized email campaigns sent over time to nurture leads and drive conversions.

### Creating a Sequence

**Manual Creation:**
1. Navigate to **Sequences**
2. Click **+ Create Sequence**
3. Enter sequence details:
   - **Name**: Descriptive title
   - **Description**: Purpose and goals
4. Add email steps:
   - **Subject Line**: Email subject
   - **Body**: Email content (supports variables)
   - **Wait Time**: Days to wait before next step
5. Click **Save Sequence**

**AI-Generated Sequences:**
1. Click **Generate with AI**
2. Describe your sequence goals
3. AI generates complete sequence with multiple steps
4. Review and edit as needed
5. Save sequence

### Sequence Steps

Each step in a sequence includes:
- **Subject Line**: Email subject with variable support
- **Email Body**: HTML or plain text content
- **Wait Time**: Delay before next step (1-30 days)
- **Conditions**: Optional sending conditions

**Available Variables:**
- `{{first_name}}`: Contact first name
- `{{last_name}}`: Contact last name
- `{{company_name}}`: Company name
- `{{job_title}}`: Contact job title
- `{{custom_field}}`: Any custom field

### Personalizing Sequences

**For Companies:**
1. Navigate to **Company Sequences**
2. Click **Generate Sequence** for a company
3. AI analyzes company data and creates personalized sequence
4. Review and customize
5. Start sequence

**For Contacts:**
1. Select contacts from People section
2. Click **Add to Sequence**
3. Choose sequence
4. AI personalizes each email based on contact data
5. Confirm and start

### Managing Active Sequences

**View Active Sequences:**
- See all contacts currently in sequences
- Track progress through sequence steps
- Monitor engagement (opens, clicks, replies)

**Pause/Resume:**
- Pause individual contacts or entire sequences
- Resume from where they left off
- Cancel sequences to stop sending

**Analytics:**
- Open rates by step
- Click-through rates
- Reply rates
- Conversion metrics
- A/B test results

### Sequence Settings

**Daily Send Limits:**
- Set maximum emails per day
- Prevent spam filters and maintain reputation
- Recommended: 50-200 per day depending on volume

**Send Schedule:**
- Configure sending hours
- Timezone-aware scheduling
- Avoid weekends (optional)

**Auto-Stop Conditions:**
- Stop when contact replies
- Stop when deal is created
- Stop on specific triggers

### Best Practices

- **Keep it Short**: 3-5 steps is optimal
- **Provide Value**: Each email should offer something useful
- **Personalize**: Use variables and AI personalization
- **Test Subject Lines**: A/B test for better open rates
- **Monitor Engagement**: Adjust based on performance
- **Allow Opt-Out**: Always include unsubscribe option

## Auto-Response Hub

### Overview

Auto-Response Hub uses AI to automatically generate responses to incoming emails, with human review before sending.

### How It Works

1. **Incoming Email Detected**: System monitors connected inboxes
2. **AI Analysis**: AI reads email and generates appropriate response
3. **Review Queue**: Response added to pending reviews
4. **Human Review**: You review and approve/edit/reject
5. **Send**: Approved responses sent automatically

### Enabling Auto-Response

1. Navigate to **Business Profile**
2. Scroll to **Auto-Response Settings**
3. Toggle **Enable Auto-Response**
4. Configure settings:
   - **Daily Limit**: Max auto-responses per day (recommended: 10-50)
   - **Response Tone**: Professional, friendly, formal, casual
   - **Auto-Send**: Enable to send without review (use cautiously)
5. Click **Save Settings**

### Reviewing Pending Responses

1. Navigate to **Auto-Response Hub**
2. View pending responses in queue
3. For each response:
   - **Preview**: See original email and AI response
   - **Edit**: Modify AI-generated content
   - **Approve**: Send response as-is
   - **Reject**: Discard response

**Bulk Actions:**
- Approve all reviewed responses
- Reject low-confidence responses
- Export for analysis

### Response Quality Indicators

Each AI response shows:
- **Confidence Score**: AI's confidence in response quality
- **Sentiment**: Detected sentiment of incoming email
- **Category**: Type of email (inquiry, support, sales)
- **Suggested Action**: Recommended next steps

### Analytics

**Auto-Response Metrics:**
- Total responses generated
- Approval rate
- Edit rate
- Send rate
- Average response time
- Engagement metrics (replies, conversions)

**Performance Tracking:**
- Response quality over time
- AI accuracy improvements
- Time saved vs manual responses

### Safety Features

- **Human-in-the-Loop**: All responses reviewed before sending (unless auto-send enabled)
- **Daily Limits**: Prevent runaway automation
- **Confidence Thresholds**: Only generate responses for clear emails
- **Spam Detection**: Automatically skip spam emails
- **Blacklist**: Exclude specific senders or domains

### Best Practices

- **Start Conservative**: Low daily limits and manual review
- **Review Regularly**: Check response quality weekly
- **Adjust Tone**: Match your brand voice
- **Monitor Engagement**: Track reply rates and satisfaction
- **Use for Common Queries**: Best for FAQs and standard inquiries
- **Human Touch**: Always review important conversations

   - **A/B Test**: Test subject lines or content

**Duplicate Campaign:**

- Verify your sending domain
- Maintain SPF, DKIM, DMARC records
- Keep bounce rate < 2%
- Monitor spam complaints
- Use double opt-in when possible

- Personalize every email
- Clear, compelling subject lines (< 50 characters)

- Single clear call-to-action
- Mobile-responsive design
- Include unsubscribe link

- Send during business hours

- Respect recipient timezones
- Avoid weekends (unless B2C)

- Monitor engagement rates
- Adjust based on results
